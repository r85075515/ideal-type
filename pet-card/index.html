FAQ

What kind of photo works best?

A clear, well-lit photo with your pet's face visible and in focus works best. Avoid blurry, very dark, or heavily filtered photos, and avoid photos where the pet's face is hidden or cut off. JPG or PNG files are fine.

How long will my cards take?

Cards are delivered digitally by email once generation and a quick quality check are complete. Ordering early in the season is recommended, as the Christmas window closes in mid-December.

Can I request changes?

If a card has an obvious error — for example, the wrong pet, a missing feature, or garbled text — contact us and we will regenerate the affected card once, free of charge. Style preferences (for example, a different Christmas theme) count as a new generation and are not included as a free revision.

What is the refund policy?

If your cards have not yet been generated, you may request a full refund. Once the cards have been delivered, refunds are not available, as this is a digital product — except where delivery fails or the files are unusable, in which case please contact us and we will make it right.

Can I use the cards commercially?

No. Finished cards are for personal use only. You may share them with family and friends or use them as a personal holiday greeting, but commercial use, resale, redistribution, and use in advertising are not permitted.

Are the cards AI-generated? Will people know?

Yes — every card is AI-generated artwork based on your pet's photo, and each card is a stylised digital illustration rather than a photograph. We recommend telling recipients the cards are AI-made when you share them.
