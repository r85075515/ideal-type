Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

What this test does

Ideal Type Finder is a small preference test. You pick between pairs of AI-generated faces; the picking runs entirely in your browser and works out which style of face you prefer.

What we collect

  • Your picks stay on your device. During this test, your choices and the test's event log are stored only in your own browser (local storage). They are not uploaded to us.
  • No accounts, no uploads. We never ask you to create an account, and there is no way to upload your own photos. No real people's photos are used anywhere in this test.
  • Payments. If you choose to buy the full result, payment is handled by Stripe on Stripe's own pages. We never see or store your card number. Stripe's handling of your payment data is covered by Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Basic hosting logs. This site is served by GitHub Pages, which may record standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

AI-generated content

Every face on this site, including any preview or delivered result, is AI-generated. No face depicts a real person.

Contact

Questions about this policy or a purchase: reply to the email receipt you received from Stripe for your purchase, and we will get back to you.
